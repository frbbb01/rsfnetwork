Privacy Policy.

Last updated: 27 September 2026

1. About this policy

RSF is an independent website and community project. This Privacy Policy explains what information RSF may collect, how it may be used, and the choices available to users.

If you have a privacy question or request, you can contact RSF through the contact page.

CONTACT RSF →

2. Information we collect

RSF is designed to collect as little personal information as reasonably possible.

RSF does not intentionally store:

  • IP addresses
  • Location information
  • Real names
  • Email addresses
  • Telephone numbers
  • Home addresses

RSF may store information necessary to operate the service, including:

  • User-created content
  • Roblox usernames
  • Discord account IDs
  • Information associated with an RSF account where necessary to operate that account

3. Optional login

Logging in to RSF is optional unless a particular feature requires an account. Users may browse publicly available parts of the website without creating an account.

4. User-created content

If you submit content to RSF, such as database information, comments, community submissions, or other material, that content may be stored and displayed as part of the service.

You should avoid submitting personal information that you do not want publicly associated with your content.

5. Roblox usernames

Roblox usernames may be stored and displayed where necessary for RSF player records, statistics, rankings, or other community features.

6. Discord account IDs

If you connect or use Discord-related functionality, RSF may receive and store a Discord account ID where necessary to provide that functionality.

7. How information is used

Information may be used to:

  • Operate and maintain RSF
  • Provide account functionality
  • Display user-created content
  • Associate content with the appropriate user
  • Maintain player, team, season, and statistical records
  • Prevent abuse and harmful activity
  • Moderate the community
  • Maintain the security and integrity of the service
  • Investigate violations of the Terms of Use
  • Comply with applicable legal obligations

8. Sale of information

RSF does not sell personal information. RSF does not intentionally create advertising profiles from user information.

9. Third-party services

RSF may rely on third-party services for hosting, authentication, databases, integrations, security, or other technical functions. Those services may process information as necessary to provide their services.

RSF may also link to third-party websites. RSF is not responsible for the privacy practices of external websites.

10. Data retention

Information may be retained for as long as reasonably necessary to provide RSF, maintain records, prevent abuse, resolve disputes, maintain security, or satisfy legal obligations.

11. Security

RSF takes reasonable measures intended to protect information from unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction. No online service can guarantee absolute security.

12. Your rights

Depending on where you live and applicable law, you may have rights concerning personal information, including rights to access, correct, delete, restrict, object to processing, or request portability of certain information.

Where processing is based on consent, you may also have the right to withdraw that consent. You may have the right to lodge a complaint with your applicable data protection authority.

CONTACT RSF →

13. Children's privacy

RSF does not intentionally collect personal information from children beyond what is reasonably necessary to provide the service. If you believe information has been submitted inappropriately, please contact RSF.

14. Changes to this policy

RSF may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be reflected on this page with an updated date.
