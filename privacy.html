Privacy Policy

Last updated: 27 September 2026

1. Who we are

RSF is an independent website and community project. For privacy-related questions, requests, or concerns, you can contact RSF through the contact page.

Contact RSF

2. Information we collect

RSF is designed to collect as little personal information as reasonably possible.

RSF does not intentionally store:

  • IP addresses
  • Location information
  • Real names
  • Email addresses
  • Telephone numbers
  • Home addresses

The information RSF may store is limited to information necessary to operate its features. This may include:

  • User-created content
  • Roblox usernames
  • Discord account IDs
  • Information associated with an RSF account where necessary to operate that account

3. Logging in is optional

You do not need to create or log into an RSF account simply to browse publicly available parts of the website.

Certain features may require an account or external account connection. Where login is optional, you can continue using portions of RSF that do not require authentication without creating an account.

4. User-created content

If you submit content to RSF, such as posts, comments, statistics, submissions, or other material, that content may be stored so that RSF can provide its features.

You should not submit information that you do not want publicly associated with your Roblox username, Discord account, or RSF account.

You are responsible for the information and material you choose to publish.

5. Roblox usernames

RSF may store Roblox usernames for purposes such as identifying players, displaying player statistics, maintaining database records, and operating RSF features.

A Roblox username is not treated by RSF as an indication that the person has provided their real-world identity.

6. Discord account IDs

Where Discord integration or authentication is used, RSF may store a Discord account ID.

A Discord account ID may be used to associate an RSF account with the corresponding Discord account and to provide account-related functionality.

7. How we use information

Information stored by RSF may be used to:

  • Operate the RSF website
  • Provide account functionality
  • Display user-created content
  • Associate content with the appropriate user or account
  • Maintain player and team records
  • Provide statistics and database features
  • Prevent abuse and harmful activity
  • Moderate content
  • Maintain the security and integrity of RSF
  • Investigate violations of the Terms of Use
  • Comply with applicable legal obligations

8. Information we do not sell

RSF does not sell users' personal information.

RSF also does not intentionally build advertising profiles from users' personal information.

9. Third-party services

RSF may rely on third-party services to operate parts of the website, such as hosting, authentication, database infrastructure, or integrations.

The website may also contain links to third-party websites. RSF is not responsible for the privacy practices of websites it does not operate.

10. Data retention

RSF keeps information only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes described in this Privacy Policy, unless a longer period is required by law.

User-created content may remain available for as long as it is needed to operate RSF or unless it is removed under RSF's moderation, deletion, or content policies.

11. Data security

RSF takes reasonable measures to protect information stored by the service against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction.

However, no internet service can guarantee absolute security.

12. Your rights

Depending on where you live and which data protection laws apply to you, you may have rights concerning your personal information, including rights to:

  • Request access to personal information held about you
  • Request correction of inaccurate information
